# Upper-Body Hypertrophy, 3 Days a Week

Full-body layout with an upper-body bias. Chest, back, and arms are trained every session. One leg movement per day. No squat rack needed. About 60 to 70 minutes per session.

## Read this first

- **Schedule:** Mon / Wed / Fri or any three non-consecutive days. Do A, B, C in order.
- **Effort:** weeks 1 to 3 stop 3 to 4 reps short of failure while you learn form. From week 4, end every set 1 to 2 reps short of failure. The last set of any machine or isolation exercise can go to failure.
- **Progression:** when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add the smallest weight increment next session and build back up. This is the rule that makes the plan work. Log every set.
- **Rest:** 2 to 3 min on presses, rows, pulldowns, and leg movements. 60 to 90 sec on isolation work.
- **Tempo:** full range of motion. Lower under control for 2 to 3 seconds. Feel the stretch at the bottom of presses, curls, and extensions.
- **Warm-up:** 5 min easy cardio, then 1 to 2 lighter ramp-up sets on the first press and first pull of the day.
- **Deload:** every 6 to 8 weeks, or when two sessions in a row go backwards, do one week at half the sets with the same weights.
- **Substitutions:** if a machine is taken, swap for the alternative listed, or anything with the same movement pattern. Keep the rep range.

## Outside the gym (this is half the result)

- **Protein:** 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of bodyweight per day, spread over 3 to 4 meals.
- **Calories:** small surplus. Aim to gain 0.25 to 0.5% of bodyweight per week. Weigh in a few mornings a week and use the average.
- **Creatine monohydrate:** 3 to 5 g every day, any time. The only supplement worth buying.
- **Sleep:** 7 to 9 hours. Keep bed and wake times consistent.
- **Alcohol:** keep it light and away from training nights.

## Weekly volume check

| Muscle | Direct sets / week | Sessions / week |
|---|---|---|
| Chest | 11 | 3 |
| Back (lats and upper back) | 15 | 3 |
| Biceps (plus indirect from 5 pulling exercises) | 9 | 3 |
| Triceps (plus indirect from 4 pressing exercises) | 9 | 3 |
| Side and rear delts | 6 | 2 |
| Legs | 9 | 3 |

These sit inside the 10 to 20 weekly sets per muscle that the volume research supports once indirect work is counted, with no session going past the point where extra sets stop paying off. If you ever add a 4th day, make it another upper session.

## Tracking

Write down weight × reps for every set. Example: chest press 50 kg, 10 / 9 / 8. Next session, aim to beat at least one of those numbers. When all three sets hit the top of the range, go up in weight.
